Scalability

Plan, build, test, and monitor Salesforce implementations at scale with AI and a suite of integrated performance tools.

The Salesforce Scalability suite provides tools to diagnose performance issues, optimize code, and validate implementations before production. These tools cover the full delivery lifecycle, supporting teams from the first line of Apex to the final major rollout. They use a shared foundation of org traffic data and AI-assisted analysis to ensure peak efficiency.

Explore Scalability

  • Agentforce for Scale
    Use conversational AI to monitor org performance and analyze results directly in Scale Test, Scale Center, and ApexGuru.
  • Scale Test
    Use metrics to plan scale tests. Book a slot in your sandbox instance calendar, and test at production peak load. Compare performance data from recent tests.
  • Scale Center
    Diagnose root causes and act on scale issues earlier in your development cycle. View org performance metrics and analyze performance reports.
